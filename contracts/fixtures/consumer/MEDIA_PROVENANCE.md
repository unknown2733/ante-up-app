# R2 sample subject media

The 15 subject names and destinations below refer to real public pages. Their five-week values, prices, calls, notes, profiles, rankings, and market phases are **fabricated examples** generated by `consumerDemo.ts`; they do not describe the subject's actual attention, audience, trading, or endorsement. Stable `demo:market:` and `demo:wallet:` IDs cannot be used as Solana addresses. The eight example profiles use locally generated geometric SVG avatars and are not real people.

`subjectUrl` identifies the subject; `imageSourceUrl` identifies the official page that shows the image. The fixture stores remote HTTPS image references, not redistributed image files. If a host disallows hotlinking or changes a URL, `SubjectImage` presents its neutral fallback. This does not grant image reuse rights outside this prototype.
