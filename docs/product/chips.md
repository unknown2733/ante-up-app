# Chips

Place a chip when you want to record a view on a market's attention. The chip action has no purchase price or cash payout. It is separate from Buy/Sell, Pro positions, and liquidity deposits.

## Place chip

On a supported Devnet market, connect the owning wallet and review **Place chip**. The app checks market identity and transaction instructions before signing. A submitted signature still needs finalization and projection into your profile. A sample item is for exploration only and cannot sign a real chip transaction.

The program permits at most **three active chips per wallet**, with **one active chip per market**. It also applies a rolling participation limit. Market readiness and other program rules can pause entry. A pending action may take time to appear in your profile.

## Take back chip

Choose **Take back chip** on an active record to finalize its attention result and free up the chip. Only its owning wallet can close it. There is no scheduled weekly forced close. If valid entry or exit data are unavailable, the result may be **N/A** instead of a percentage. The close and later proof completion are separate steps.

## Reading a result

A chip result compares attention reference values at entry and exit. It is **not money earned or lost** or a token price return. An open chip can show an unfinished current reference, excluded from the completed leaderboard. Positive, negative, pending, and N/A outcomes belong in your history.
