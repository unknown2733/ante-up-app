# FAQ

## Do I need a wallet to read Docs or Explore?

No. Explore, reference charts, and public Devnet profiles are readable without signing. Connect a compatible Devnet wallet for supported on-chain actions.

## Is a chip a token purchase?

No. Placing and taking back a chip record an attention view with no cash payout. Buy/Sell, Pro, and liquidity are separate test-asset financial flows. See [Chips](/docs/chips).

## Are all subjects live markets?

No. **Preview** subjects provide public reference data or official information and artwork. They are read-only. Only a registered, eligible market with the correct chain identity can offer an executable action. See [Markets and trading](/docs/markets-trading).

## What does a preview count measure?

Read its metric label: YouTube video views, X post likes, or Spotify track streams. The source link, UTC capture time, and any provider report date identify the value. Third-party Spotify reports keep their attribution and report date; popularity scores are not stream counts. See [Data and pricing](/docs/data-pricing).

## Why does a subject show a snapshot instead of a chart?

One captured count establishes a snapshot. A history needs multiple observations, and a change needs comparable counts at different times. Capture time records when the source was retrieved. A report may describe an earlier date, and platform counters may be cached. The snapshot does not indicate a continuous feed.

## Why is an action unavailable?

A Preview subject is read-only. A Devnet market may be on the wrong network, preparing data, low activity, recovering from an outage, in another phase, or limited by capital and operator rules. Read the action area's current reason.

## Why is my result N/A?

A valid entry or exit attention reference may be unavailable. N/A is a recorded outcome, not a cash loss. It differs from an open chip's unfinished current reference.

## Why does a proof still say pending?

Closing a chip and issuing evidence proof are separate operations. Storage verification and SAS issuance may take longer or need a worker retry. Inspect the record's proof link for its current state. Pending proof is not issued proof. See [Records and proofs](/docs/records-proofs).

## How do Devnet SOL and tUSD differ?

SOL pays network fees. tUSD is the app's test payment token for supported financial actions. Faucet amount and limits depend on current configuration. See [Wallet and faucet](/docs/wallet-faucet).

## Is the leaderboard a trading return ranking?

No. It ranks eligible completed chip attention results from actual Devnet records; N/A and open records are handled separately. Attention change is not monetary profit and loss.
