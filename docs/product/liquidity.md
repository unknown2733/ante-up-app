# Liquidity

Liquidity participation is a test-asset financial flow. Depositing tUSD into a supported market's vault differs from placing a no-money chip. The vault reserves for trading liabilities; a deposit does not guarantee income or immediate withdrawal.

## Deposit liquidity

Review the market, amount, vault state, quote, and wallet prompt. The program can restrict deposits by phase, admission, configured caps, and settlement obligations. An Explore item may have no executable liquidity route.

## Withdraw liquidity

**Withdraw liquidity** follows program windows, available funds, and liability rules. An apparent balance is not an unconditional claim on all vault assets. Withdrawal may wait while obligations are reserved or a window is closed. Confirm the current quote and finalized chain result before treating funds as returned.

The MVP has a custom reserve based vault. It does not implement InfinityPools style borrowing or reconstruction of external AMM liquidity. Test-asset behavior cannot establish real asset safety.
