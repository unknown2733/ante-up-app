# Markets and trading

Explore has one catalog. Read each item's provenance and action state before treating a chart as market data. A real subject can have illustrative numbers; its name and artwork do not create a live feed, on-chain pool, or permission to trade.

## Reading a market

Look for the source label, observation time, market phase, and enabled actions. **Sample history** means synthetic chart values. **Example activity** means fictional actors and actions. These stay out of real wallet records, the Devnet leaderboard, and settlement. The collected Devnet `solana` keyword is the previously verified live market in this snapshot; other subjects do not imply new X collection or registration.

Data can be preparing, current, low activity, or unavailable. New paid entry may be blocked during a gap or recovery while an existing exit remains allowed. The action area explains unavailability. A real API failure must not silently show a sample record instead.

## Buy and sell

**Buy** and **Sell** exchange test assets on an executable market. Before graduation, the program uses a bonding curve. After graduation, spot exchange uses the index path. These are financial trades, unlike chips. Quotes expire and may change with data or market state. Review amounts, fees, market, and wallet instructions before signing.

Registration and graduation are separate operations subject to program and operator rules. A catalog item alone is not a registered market. The MVP uses a custom Anchor program and a reserve based settlement design; it does not borrow or reconstruct external AMM liquidity.
