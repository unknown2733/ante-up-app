# Getting started

Ante Up lets you follow a subject's attention, place a chip on a view, and keep a record of the result. **Call it. Ante up. Prove it.**

## Explore without a wallet

Open [Explore](/markets) and choose a subject. Observed Devnet markets show collected attention data and available actions. **Preview** subjects offer captured platform counts or official information and artwork. Read each metric, source, capture time, and any provider report date. A single count appears as a snapshot; previews are read-only.

## Place your first chip

On an executable Devnet market, connect a wallet on the matching network and choose **Place chip**. This no-money action records an attention view; it does not buy a token or create a cash position. An open chip appears under [Active chips](/me). Choose **Take back chip** to finalize its result and free up the chip. Read [Chips](/docs/chips) for limits and result states.

## Trade only when you mean to

The [wallet and faucet guide](/docs/wallet-faucet) explains Devnet SOL and test tUSD. Buy/Sell, Pro Long/Short, and liquidity are separate financial flows using test assets, quotes, and wallet authorization. Availability depends on market state and operator limits.

## Check the record

Your [chip history](/me) and [record guide](/docs/records-proofs) distinguish an on-chain result from evidence storage and later proof issuance. A pending proof does not mean a finalized close failed. Profiles, activity, and rankings use actual Devnet records.
